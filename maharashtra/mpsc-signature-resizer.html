MPSC Signature — Frequently Asked Questions

The MPSC portal requires a signature image of 140 × 60 pixels in JPEG format with a maximum file size of 20 KB. The signature should be on a plain white background.
Use black or blue ink on plain white paper. Avoid gel pens that smear. Sign naturally — do not print or trace your signature. Photograph the signature in bright natural light or scan it for best clarity.
At 140×60 px, a JPEG at 80–90% quality is typically 5–15 KB. If it is still over 20 KB, lower the quality slider to 70%. The small canvas size means even moderate quality is more than sufficient visually. The validation strip will show green when you are within the limit.
