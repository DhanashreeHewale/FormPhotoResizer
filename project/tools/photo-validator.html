Photo Validator — FAQs

It checks 6 things: (1) pixel width, (2) pixel height, (3) file size in KB, (4) file format (JPG vs PNG), (5) aspect ratio, and (6) background colour. Each check shows pass ✅, fail ❌, or warning ⚠️ with a one-click fix link if needed.
The validator samples the corner pixels of your photo to estimate background colour. If they are not close to white (#FFFFFF), it flags a warning. Most government portals require a white background. Use the AI Background Remover to fix this instantly.
