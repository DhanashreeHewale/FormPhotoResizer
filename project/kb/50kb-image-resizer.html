Resize Image to 50 KB — FAQs

50 KB is the most common photo size limit across Indian government portals. Aadhar Card (UIDAI), MPSC, Maharashtra Police Bharti, SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, ZP Bharti, and many state PSC exams all require photos of 50 KB or less.
For passport-sized dimensions (125–200 px wide), JPEG quality of 75–85% consistently produces files between 15–45 KB. Start at 82% (the default). If the output is still over 50 KB, reduce to 72–75%. The live KB meter will show you exactly where you stand.

At passport-sized dimensions, JPEG compression to 50 KB retains more than sufficient quality for identity verification. Government portals do not need print-quality images — they need a clear, recognisable face. Quality at 75–85% is invisible to the human eye at these small sizes.
