SSC Photo — Frequently Asked Questions

SSC CGL requires a photograph of 275 × 354 pixels, JPEG format, maximum 50 KB, and a signature of 236 × 79 pixels, maximum 20 KB. The background must be plain white and the face must be clearly visible with no accessories like sunglasses or caps. Steno and Selection Post use the same photo and signature size as CGL.
SSC CHSL requires a photograph of 200 × 240 pixels, max 50 KB, and a signature of 200 × 80 pixels, max 20 KB. Use the SSC CHSL Photo and SSC CHSL Signature presets above — they pre-fill these exact values and auto-name the files.
SSC GD Constable and SSC MTS both require a photograph of 200 × 240 pixels (max 50 KB), a signature of 240 × 80 pixels (max 20 KB), and a left thumb impression of 240 × 240 pixels (max 50 KB). For the thumb impression, ink your left thumb and press it on plain white paper, then photograph it in good light — the tool will resize it to the exact required dimensions without applying face-detection cropping.
