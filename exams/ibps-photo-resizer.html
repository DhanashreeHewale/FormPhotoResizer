IBPS PO, Clerk, SO and RRB all require a photograph of 200 × 230 pixels, JPEG format, maximum 50 KB. The background must be plain white and the face should cover 70–80% of the frame with eyes clearly open.
Both the signature and left thumb impression must be 140 × 60 pixels, JPEG format, maximum 20 KB. For the signature, use black or blue ink on plain white paper. For the thumb impression, use a clean stamp pad — press firmly on white paper and photograph in good light with no smearing.
